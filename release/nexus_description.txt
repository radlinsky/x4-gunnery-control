[b]X4 Gunnery Control[/b]

Turn the Access Gunnery Control chair into a turret console. Choose your turret groups, direct their fire, and watch from turret or target cameras.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pn1kFxXdgzU

[b]Controls[/b]

[list]
[*]Select turret groups using checkboxes or the rotatable, zoomable ship hologram. Markers show selection, firing and hits; missile turrets do not report hits.
[*][b]Auto-engage:[/b] watch your selected turrets while they choose their own targets. No fire orders are issued.
[*][b]Direct-control:[/b] choose a ship, station, turret, shield or engine for your selected groups to attack. Click a turret in the hologram to switch cameras.
[*][b]Attack all enemies:[/b] prefer your chosen target, with other enemies as fallback. [b]Attack my current enemy:[/b] stick to your chosen target.
[*]Filter surface elements by type or equipment. Live [b]IN RANGE[/b] counts estimate how many selected operational turrets can reach each target; they do not guarantee a clear line of fire or that a turret will fire.
[*][b]Auto-next:[/b] replace destroyed targets automatically, trying other surfaces on the same ship or station, then its hull, then eligible nearby enemies. Turn it off for manual selection.
[/list]

Previous turret settings are restored when you leave the chair. Click [b]Update turret behavior[/b] on the main console to keep your current Mode/Armed settings. Saving and loading preserves your session.

[b]Camera views[/b]

Choose Turret or Target POV, with manual or cinematic views. Hold Shift + middle mouse button to look around in manual views. Cinematic views hide the UI; Esc returns to the manual panel. Previous/Next Turret changes camera focus.

[b]Requirements and installation[/b]

X4 Foundations 9.00 or newer, plus:

[list]
[*][b]kuertee UI Extensions and HUD:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3477279743]Steam[/url] / [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/x4foundations/mods/552]Nexus[/url]. Keep Protected UI Mode active.
[*][b]Print Extension List:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3770927339]Steam[/url] / [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/x4foundations/mods/2191]Nexus[/url].
[/list]

Steam: subscribe to Gunnery Control and its required extensions. Nexus/Vortex: install the requirements, then the mod ZIP. Manual: extract to X4 Foundations/extensions/x4_gunnery_control/. Enable the extensions in-game.

Optional: SirNukes Mod Support APIs lets you open the console while on foot aboard your own ship: Map → right-click that ship → Gunnery Control.

[b]Limitations[/b]

[list]
[*]Direct-control selects targets; it does not provide manual aiming.
[*]Some S/M ships use a ship camera. Turret cinematic views can clip into the hull.
[*]Duplicate-named turret groups can mislabel members; commands still reach the correct group.
[*]If Esc stops responding after a camera session, open and close the Map.
[/list]

[b]Reporting problems[/b]

Add this to your launch options:
[code]-debug all -logfile debug.log[/code]
Reproduce the issue, quit, and attach debug.log to a [url=https://github.com/radlinsky/x4-gunnery-control/issues]GitHub issue[/url]. On Windows it is usually in:
[code]C:\Users\<your-name>\Documents\Egosoft\X4\<number>\debug.log[/code]
Save the log before launching X4 again; the next launch replaces it.
