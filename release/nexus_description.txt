[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]

Makes the [b]Access Gunnery Control[/b] chair worth sitting in. Instead of the vanilla secondary-control menu, you get a full turret console, direct fire orders, and four camera views of your turrets at work.

[size=4][b]What you can do[/b][/size]

[b]Pick your turret groups.[/b] Sitting down opens the console. Every turret group gets a checkbox; check the ones you want to work with, or use [b]Select all[/b]. You can change each group's [b]Mode[/b] and [b]Armed[/b] state right there, exactly like on the ship's behaviour tab.

Once you select at least one group, two buttons light up:

[b]Auto-engage[/b]: does not alter turret behavior at all. Your turrets keep choosing targets by whatever settings the ship already had. This mode exists purely so you can sit and watch them work.

[b]Direct-control[/b]: you tell the checked groups exactly what to shoot. The [b]Select Engagement Target[/b] menu lists the ships and stations in radar range; click one and its hull is engaged immediately, but you can instead mark a specific surface element (i.e. turret, shield, engine).

By default in Direct-control, your checked groups focus only your chosen target.
If that target is out of range for some checked turrets, those turrets can sit
idle.

In Direct-control, you can also use [b]All Turrets: Prefer My Target[/b] to push
every combat turret on the ship to prefer your target; turrets that cannot hit
it can still shoot other valid in-range hostiles.
Then use [b]Release Other Turrets[/b] to turn that ship-wide preference back
off: non-selected turrets return to normal logic, and selected turrets go back
to default Direct-control behavior (which can mean idle when your chosen target
is out of range).

[list]
[*][b]Auto-next Target when destroyed[/b] is checked by default: when the target dies, the turret(s) and camera automatically select the next target. Uncheck it, and the turret(s) will sit idly by until you pick the next target yourself, which is closer to actually manning the gun.[/*]
[*][b]Next Target[/b] / [b]Previous Target[/b] step through the same candidate list without reopening the browser.[/*]
[/list]
[size=4][b]Four viewing modes[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Turret POV manual[/b] / [b]Target POV manual[/b] — camera on the turret, or on what it is shooting at, with the normal UI still up. Hold [b]Shift[/b] + middle mouse button to look around freely.[/*]
[*][b]Turret POV cinematic[/b] / [b]Target POV cinematic[/b] — the same two viewpoints through the game's cutscene camera. It hides all UI and aims the camera for you. Press [b]Esc[/b] to come back to the manual panel.[/*]
[*][b]Next Turret[/b] / [b]Previous Turret[/b] cycle the camera through every operational turret in the checked groups. This only moves the camera.[/*]
[/list]
[size=4][b]Limitations[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]This is not manual aiming.[/b] Direct-control tells the turrets [i]what[/i] to hit, not [i]how[/i]. The engine exposes no way to man a turret yourself.[/*]
[*][b]Turret POV cinematic often clips the camera into your own hull.[/b] It looks rough. Target POV cinematic is the better-looking of the two.[/*]
[*]If you have turned help texts off in the game options, the confirmation popup cannot appear — and it also happens to work around an X4 bug where [b]Esc[/b] goes dead after a camera session. Without it, press [b]Esc[/b] once after standing up; it stays unresponsive until any other menu opens and closes (i.e. M for map).[/*]
[*]On some S/M ships, turret target view can resolve to a wider ship-level
camera framing instead of a tight turret component view.[/*]
[*]If two turret groups share the same name, some member rows or camera picks
can appear under the sibling label. Commands still apply to the correct group.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[b]Vortex[/b]: install UI Extensions and HUD first, then install the ZIP without changing its top-level [b]x4_gunnery_control[/b] folder.

[b]Manual[/b]: extract the archive so you end up with [b]X4 Foundations/extensions/x4_gunnery_control/[/b], then enable both extensions in the Extensions menu and load a save.
