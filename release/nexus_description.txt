[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]

Makes the [b]Access Gunnery Control[/b] chair worth sitting in. Instead of the vanilla secondary-control menu, you get a full turret console, direct fire orders, and four camera views of your turrets at work.

[size=4][b]What you can do[/b][/size]

[b]Pick your turret groups.[/b] Sitting down opens the console. Every
turret group gets a checkbox; check the ones you want to work with,
or use [b]Select all[/b]. You can change each
group's [b]Mode[/b] and [b]Armed[/b] state right there, exactly like on the
ship's behaviour tab.

Once you select at least one group, two buttons light up:

[b]Auto-engage[/b]: issues no fire orders — your turrets still choose their own targets. Before
entering the camera it writes your checked groups' staged console settings to the
ship (so ticking a group, which sets its Mode to [b]Attack all enemies[/b], does take
effect for the duration). The camera cycles only through the turrets of the
checked groups, not every turret on the ship. Everything is reverted when you get
up.

[b]Direct-control[/b]: you tell the checked groups exactly what to shoot.
The [b]Select Engagement Target[/b] menu lists the ships and stations in radar range; click
one and its hull is engaged immediately, but you can instead
mark a specific surface element (e.g. turret, shield, engine).

[b]Engageable ratio.[/b] Gunnery Control shows an [b]N / total ENGAGEABLE[/b] count for
your selected target. This is a mod-computed geometry check — not a guarantee
the turret will fire. The numerator counts turrets that currently have a clean
shot: known traverse arc covering the aim direction, target within weapon range,
and a clear line of fire past your own ship. Unknown-arc turrets stay in the
denominator and are reported separately as [b]UNKNOWN[/b]. Readiness, fire
authorization, an intercept solution, and actual firing are all separate states
that this ratio does not measure.

[list]
[*][b]Auto-next Target when destroyed[/b] is checked by default: when the target dies, the turret(s) and camera automatically select the next target. Uncheck it, and the turret(s) will sit idly by until you pick the next target yourself, which is closer to actually manning the gun.[/*]
[*][b]Next Target[/b] / [b]Previous Target[/b] step through the same candidate list without reopening the browser.[/*]
[/list]
Everything you change in the console is temporary and undone when you press [b]Get Up[/b] — unless you press [b]Update turret behavior[/b] first, which makes those changes permanent.

Saving and loading keeps your seat: load a save while engaged and the game puts you back at the same turret, watching the same target, with the same groups checked.

[size=4][b]Four viewing modes[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Turret POV manual[/b] / [b]Target POV manual[/b] — camera on the turret, or on what it is shooting at, with the normal UI still up. Hold [b]Shift[/b] + middle mouse button to look around freely.[/*]
[*][b]Turret POV cinematic[/b] / [b]Target POV cinematic[/b] — the same two viewpoints through the game's cutscene camera. It hides all UI and aims the camera for you. Press [b]Esc[/b] to come back to the manual panel.[/*]
[*][b]Next Turret[/b] / [b]Previous Turret[/b] cycle the camera through every operational turret in the checked groups. This only moves the camera.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Limitations[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]This is not manual aiming.[/b] Direct-control tells the turrets [i]what[/i] to hit, not [i]how[/i]. The engine exposes no way to man a turret yourself.[/*]
[*][b]On small ships the camera watches the whole ship, not the turret.[/b] X4 resolves a turret camera on S/M ships to the ship itself. Everything still works — Direct-control, target selection, auto-engage — the view is just less specific than on a capital ship.[/*]
[*][b]Turret POV cinematic often clips the camera into your own hull.[/b] It looks rough. Target POV cinematic is the better-looking of the two.[/*]
[*]The confirmation popup that appears when you stand up also works around an X4 bug that leaves [b]Esc[/b] dead after a camera session. If you have turned help texts off in the game options, that popup never shows — and the workaround goes with it. Press [b]Esc[/b] once after standing up; it stays unresponsive until any other menu opens and closes (e.g. M for map).[/*]
[*][b]The Engageable ratio is a targeting aid, not a firing guarantee.[/b] It checks geometry only. A turret can show ENGAGEABLE and still hold fire because it is not ready, lacks authorization, or cannot solve the intercept. Conversely, [b]UNKNOWN[/b] turrets may still fire if their arc data are unavailable even though they could shoot.[/*]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]X4 Foundations 9.00 or newer.[/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/x4foundations/mods/552]kuertee UI Extensions and HUD[/url]. Leave Protected UI Mode active as its instructions recommend.[/*]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/x4foundations/mods/2191]Print Extension List[/url].[/*]
[/list]
This extension replaces no vanilla game files, no UI Extensions files, and no combat AI scripts.

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[b]Vortex[/b]: install the two required extensions first, then install the ZIP without changing its top-level [b]x4_gunnery_control[/b] folder.

[b]Manual[/b]: extract the archive so you end up with [b]X4 Foundations/extensions/x4_gunnery_control/[/b], then enable all installed extensions in the Extensions menu and load a save.
